# 🌴 SAWIT PANIC!

### Harvest Fast. Build Combos. Make Every Decision Count.

**SAWIT PANIC!** is a mobile-first HTML5 arcade game that combines fast-paced harvesting, skill-based scoring, and environmental decision-making in a vibrant tropical plantation.

Meet **SawitMan**, a cheerful oil palm fruit mascot, and take on the challenge of harvesting ripe fruit bunches while avoiding costly mistakes.

Every harvest counts. Every second matters. And every decision shapes your plantation.

## 🎮 About the Game

SAWIT PANIC! is a browser-based casual arcade game designed around simple touch interactions, responsive gameplay, and replayable scoring challenges.

Players must identify and harvest ripe oil palm fruit bunches, maintain combo streaks, manage limited lives, and make strategic plantation decisions.

The game blends colorful arcade entertainment with an environmental awareness theme, encouraging players to consider the consequences of land development choices.

## 🕹️ How to Play

### 1. Harvest the Right Fruit

Tap ripe orange-red oil palm fruit bunches to earn points.

Avoid harvesting unripe green fruit or overripe brown fruit. Incorrect harvests reduce your score and cost one heart.

### 2. Build Your Combo

Successful consecutive harvests increase your score multiplier.

| Combo Streak | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | x1 |
| 5–9 | x2 |
| 10+ | x3 |

A harvesting mistake resets your combo.

### 3. Make Strategic Choices

During your session, you will encounter plantation management decisions.

**Expand Land** — Gain additional harvesting opportunities at an environmental cost.

**Burn Forest** — Expand aggressively, but face major environmental consequences and scoring penalties.

**Upgrade Plantation** — Improve productivity while supporting environmental sustainability.

Each choice includes a visual outcome sequence before its gameplay effects are applied.

### 4. Unlock Bonus Harvest

Reach the cumulative target of **30 successful harvests** to activate Bonus Harvest Mode.

Starting with your 31st successful harvest, earn an additional **50 flat bonus points** per harvest.

The bonus does not multiply with combo or temporary score boosts.

### 5. Beat Your Personal Best

You have **75 seconds** and **three hearts** to achieve the highest score possible.

Master the harvesting rhythm, build long combo streaks, and choose your strategy wisely.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Requirements

A modern browser with JavaScript enabled, such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

### Run Locally

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/nugrohoiif/Sawit-Panic-Mini-Game.git
```

Open the project directory:

```bash
cd Sawit-Panic-Mini-Game
```

Open the game's HTML entry point in your browser.

For development using a local server:

```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit:

```text
http://localhost:8000
```

*Note: The exact launch path depends on where the playable HTML file is located in the repository.*

## 🎨 Art & Game Design Philosophy

SAWIT PANIC! follows a **Cute Tropical** visual direction, featuring warm tropical colors, playful characters, glossy arcade-inspired UI elements, and expressive gameplay feedback.

The game is designed around three principles:

**Clarity** — Players should immediately understand what can be tapped, what earns points, and what causes penalties.

**Responsiveness** — Every meaningful interaction should provide clear visual or audio feedback.

**Meaningful Choices** — Strategic decisions should have visible consequences that affect the gameplay experience.
